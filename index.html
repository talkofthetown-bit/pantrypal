Questions

Good questions.

Do I need every ingredient?

No. Recipes marked "Ready now" use only what you have. We also show near matches and list exactly what is missing.

How accurate are the calories?

They are estimates per serving using typical portion sizes. Your own amounts and brands will change the numbers a little.

Does it count oil, salt and water?

No. We assume you have salt, pepper, cooking oil and water, so you never have to add them.

Can I filter for vegetarian or vegan?

Yes. Pick a diet, a calorie limit or a max cooking time and the list updates straight away.
